## Landmark / foundational

### 1. Self-Assembly at All Scales
- **Authors:** George M. Whitesides, Bartosz Grzybowski · **Year:** 2002 · **Venue:** *Science* 295(5564), 2418–2421
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1070821 · [Open PDF (course mirror)](https://courses.cs.duke.edu/spring02/cps296.5/papers/WhitesidesSelfAssembly.pdf)
- **Takeaways:** Self-assembly = organization without human intervention; static vs dynamic; interactions + design set what forms; biology already does this spectacularly.
- **Why beginners:** Best single conceptual map across chemistry, materials, and robotics.

### 2. Nucleic acid junctions and lattices
- **Authors:** Nadrian C. Seeman · **Year:** 1982 · **Venue:** *Journal of Theoretical Biology* 99(2), 237–247
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-5193(82)90002-9 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6188926/)
- **Takeaways:** DNA as building material; sticky-end complementarity; founded structural DNA nanotechnology.
- **Why beginners:** The “why DNA?” origin story.

### 3. Design and self-assembly of two-dimensional DNA crystals
- **Authors:** Erik Winfree, Furong Liu, Lisa A. Wenzler, Nadrian C. Seeman · **Year:** 1998 · **Venue:** *Nature* 394, 539–544
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1038/28998
- **Takeaways:** DNA DX molecules as Wang tiles; algorithmic self-assembly experimentally real; errors already visible.
- **Why beginners:** Bridge between CS algorithms and wet nanotech.

### 4. Folding DNA to create nanoscale shapes and patterns
- **Authors:** Paul W. K. Rothemund · **Year:** 2006 · **Venue:** *Nature* 440, 297–302
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04586 · [Author PDF](https://www.dna.caltech.edu/Papers/DNAorigami-nature.pdf)
- **Takeaways:** Scaffolded DNA origami; ~100 nm shapes; ~6 nm pixels; CAD-like workflow.
- **Why beginners:** The visually iconic, teachable demo.

### 5. Three-Dimensional Structures Self-Assembled from DNA Bricks
- **Authors:** Yonggang Ke, Luvena L. Ong, William M. Shih, Peng Yin · **Year:** 2012 · **Venue:** *Science* 338(6111), 1177–1183
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1227268 · [Author PDF](https://yin.hms.harvard.edu/publications/2012.bricks.pdf)
- **Takeaways:** Short DNA bricks as LEGO voxels; modular addressable 3D canvas.
- **Why beginners:** Second major programming style besides origami.

### 6. Programmable Matter
- **Authors:** Seth Copen Goldstein, Jason D. Campbell, Todd C. Mowry · **Year:** 2005 · **Venue:** *IEEE Computer* 38(6), 99–101
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1109/MC.2005.198 · [Project page](http://www.cs.cmu.edu/~claytronics/papers/goldstein-computer05.html)
- **Takeaways:** Catoms vision; synthetic reality; hardware/algorithms/power co-equal; still largely unrealized.
- **Why beginners:** Clear honest vision for modular path.

### 7. Programmable self-assembly in a thousand-robot swarm
- **Authors:** Michael Rubenstein, Alejandro Cornejo, Radhika Nagpal · **Year:** 2014 · **Venue:** *Science* 345(6198), 795–799
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1254295 · [News](https://seas.harvard.edu/news/self-organizing-thousand-robot-swarm)
- **Takeaways:** 1,024 Kilobots; local communication; seed + edge-follow; robustness to failures.
- **Why beginners:** Most inspiring visible robotics demo of engineered self-assembly.

### 8. Kilobot platform
- **Authors:** Michael Rubenstein, Christian Ahler, Radhika Nagpal · **Year:** 2014 (RAS; also ICRA 2012)
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.robot.2013.08.006
- **Takeaways:** Low-cost swarm platform; simplify the robot so the collective can be the experiment.

### 9. Theory of algorithmic self-assembly
- **Authors:** David Doty · **Year:** 2012 · **Venue:** *Communications of the ACM* 55(12), 78–88
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1145/2380656.2380675 · [Author PDF](https://www.dna.caltech.edu/Papers/algorithmic_tiles2012doty.pdf)
- **Takeaways:** aTAM; self-assembly can compute; error correction central; CS-friendly vocabulary.

### 10. Modular Reconfigurable Robotics
- **Authors:** Jungwon Seo, Jamie Paik, Mark Yim · **Year:** 2019 · **Venue:** *Annu. Rev. Control Robot. Auton. Syst.* 2, 63–88
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-control-053018-023834
- **Takeaways:** Lattice/chain/mobile/hybrid taxonomy; open challenges, not mature products.

### 11. Autonomous self-reconfiguration methods survey
- **Authors:** Pierre Thalamy, Benoît Piranda, Julien Bourgeois · **Year:** 2019 · **Venue:** *Robotics and Autonomous Systems*
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.robot.2019.07.012
- **Takeaways:** Algorithm-first view toward programmable matter; scale still far from vision.

### 12. Particle robotics
- **Authors:** Shuguang Li, Richa Batra, David Brown, et al. (incl. Daniela Rus, Hod Lipson) · **Year:** 2019 · **Venue:** *Nature* 569, 361–365
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1022-9 · [Lab](https://www.creativemachineslab.com/particle-robotics.html)
- **Takeaways:** Loosely coupled expand/contract units; robustness via redundancy; contrast to precise docking.

## Reviews & broader surveys

### 13. From Conventional to Programmable Matter Systems (ACM CSUR 2024)
- **Authors:** Ahmed Amine Chafik, Jaafar Gaber, Souad Tayane, Mohamed Ennaji, Julien Bourgeois, Tarek El Ghazawi
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3653671
- **Takeaways:** Modern survey of materials, devices, control; treat as frontier overview.

### 14. Decoding Modular Reconfigurable Robots (arXiv 2023 / IJRR 2024)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.09743 · **Journal DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1177/02783649241283847
- **Takeaways:** Mechanism-centric taxonomy (connectors, actuators); why lab demos often don’t transfer.

## Molecular / colloidal frontier

### 15. Assembly of Complex Colloidal Systems Using DNA (2024)
- **Authors:** William M. Jacobs, W. Benjamin Rogers · **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08988
- **Takeaways:** Kinetics dominate; narrow temperature windows; inverse design of pathways still open.

### 16. Pattern recognition in nucleation kinetics (Nature 2024)
- **Authors:** Constantine G. Evans, Jackson O’Brien, Erik Winfree, Arvind Murugan
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06890-z · [Supp](https://www.dna.caltech.edu/SupplementaryMaterial/MultifariousSST/)
- **Takeaways:** Multifarious tile library; nucleation as classifier; research-lab only.

### 17. Dynamic DNA superstructures with emergent functions (2025)
- **Authors:** Duke, Sterling, Teng, Altunina, Martynenko, Ke, Castro, Arya
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1039/D5NH00436E
- **Takeaways:** Reconfigurability + hierarchical assembly; state-of-art 2025 pointer.

### 18. Programming DNA machines to move (Nat. Rev. Chem. 2026)
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41570-025-00791-7
- **Takeaways:** Motors/walkers/switches vocabulary; large gap vs biological motors; demystifies nanorobot hype.

### 19. Lipid vesicle-based molecular robots (Lab on a Chip 2024)
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1039/D3LC00860F
- **Takeaways:** Vesicles as soft bodies; sensing/computation/actuation integration still early.

### 20. DNA-based nanoparticle assembly (Mirkin et al. 1996)
- **Authors:** Chad A. Mirkin, Robert L. Letsinger, Robert C. Mucic, James J. Storhoff
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1038/382607a0
- **Takeaways:** Foundational DNA-programmed colloidal materials; root of programmable sticky colloids.
