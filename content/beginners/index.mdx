# Beginners track

Five short chapters. Plain sentences. Pictures and real lab demos. No fake papers or fake videos.

<Callout type="warning" emoji="⚗️">
**Honest framing.** Engineered self-assembly is mostly **lab-scale**. Nature already self-assembles. Engineers program interactions. Famous demos prove principles. Mass-market “programmable matter factories” do not exist yet. These chapters teach *how to think and how to start* — kits, makerspaces, small CNC/3D print, and educational wet-lab services — not how to open a factory.
</Callout>

## Who this is for

Curious high-school and early-college students. Makers. Anyone who wants a concrete path before diving into papers.
