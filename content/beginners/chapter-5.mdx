# Chapter 5 — Your first safe experiments

Theory sticks when you **measure something**. This chapter points you into the lab pages — safely.

## Ground rules

- No hazardous chemistry, open flames for chemistry, high voltage, pressurized gases, or biological agents at home.
- Prefer kitchen/tabletop physics and simulations.
- DNA wet work → supervised university / core facility only.
- Strong neodymium magnets: pinch hazard; keep away from pacemakers and magnetic media.

## Mini checklist before you start

1. Write a one-sentence question (“Do clumps form faster with more agitation?”).
2. List materials you already have.
3. Decide what you will **count or time** (not just “looks cool”).
4. Take before/after photos.
5. Note one failure mode.
