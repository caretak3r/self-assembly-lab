## Highlights

### 1. Orientational Ordering of Janus Particle Arrays as Classical Compass Spin Systems
- **Authors:** Zhijie Fan, Myeonggon Park, Marija Vucelja, Gia-Wei Chern
- **Submitted:** 2026-09-24
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30025
- **Why newsworthy:** Classical 120° compass and Kern–Frenkel models for triangular metallodielectric Janus arrays. Monte Carlo shows sixfold order via thermal order-by-disorder, with an intermediate quasi-long-range regime consistent with two BKT transitions — matching the experimental motif of sixfold order plus vortices/antivortices.
- **Beginner hook:** Two-faced beads on a grid act like compass needles — heat can pick a preferred pattern from many equal-looking options.
- **Caveat:** Theory / Monte Carlo (experiment-motivated).

### 2. Speed up of passive tracers in mixtures with active chemical reactions
- **Authors:** Filipe C. Thewes, Chengjie Luo, David Zwicker
- **Submitted:** 2026-09-24
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29416
- **Why newsworthy:** Thermodynamically consistent reactions open a relaxation pathway that suppresses interaction-induced memory drag and restores tracer diffusivity; actively driven reactions can push diffusivity *beyond* the empty-solvent limit (confirmed in particle simulations).
- **Beginner hook:** Chemistry that converts particles can make a wandering probe move *faster*, not slower.
- **Caveat:** Theory + simulation.

### 3. Reprogrammable origami through bistable buckled hinges
- **Authors:** Leon M. Kamp, Lucy Liu, Ella McRitchie, Damien Rouchouse, Orel Mazor, Davood Farhadi, L. Mahadevan, Katia Bertoldi
- **Submitted:** 2026-09-23
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28790
- **Why newsworthy:** Bistable buckled shims across origami hinges create an asymmetric torque–angle preference for mountain vs valley. Flipping a shim reprograms that hinge after fabrication; self-switching hinges let global boundary inputs rewrite the folding pathway.
- **Beginner hook:** Flip tiny memory springs in the hinges so the same crease pattern prefers a different 3D shape.
- **Caveat:** Soft-matter mechanics / experiment — we propose only dry paper/card demos, not lab fabrication of buckled shims.

### 4. Superadiabatic Dynamical Density Functional Theory for One-Dimensional Brownian Hard-Rod Fluids
- **Authors:** Jens Weimar, Daniel de las Heras, Martin Oettel
- **Submitted:** 2026-09-24
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29838
- **Why newsworthy:** Superadiabatic DDFT with exact equilibrium excess free energy and a three-particle adiabatic closure matches Brownian-dynamics superadiabatic response when initial ensembles align; a velocity-gradient power functional also works with a simulation memory kernel.
- **Beginner hook:** When hard beads rearrange, extra nonequilibrium forces appear that sleepy equilibrium recipes miss — this theory catches them in a clean 1D testbed.
- **Caveat:** Theory / simulation.

### 5. Boundary and bulk perturbations in vectorial active matter
- **Authors:** Giuseppe Fava
- **Submitted:** 2026-09-23
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28593
- **Why newsworthy:** Thesis on how explicit rotational-symmetry breaking — global environmental anisotropy or boundary-induced local anisotropies — reshapes dry flocking relative to free bulk collective motion.
- **Beginner hook:** Walls and preferred “wind” directions are not decorations — they change how active flocks dance.
- **Caveat:** Theory thesis overview.
