## Highlights

### 1. Active spinners drive passive matter into chiral rotors
- **Authors:** Dennis Schorn, Anpuj Nair S, Stijn van der Ham, Benno Liebchen, Hanumantha Rao Vutukuri
- **Submitted:** 2026-09-30
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39439
- **Why newsworthy:** Purely rotational magnetic spinners transfer chirality and edge currents to passive colloids; changing composition/attraction flips the organization inside-out so passive clusters rotate (with shared |Ω|∼R⁻² scaling) inside a chiral spinner fluid.
- **Beginner hook:** Spinning beads that never swim can still lend handedness and rim currents to quiet beads.
- **Caveat:** Magnetic-spinner / colloidal experiment + simulation.

### 2. Fully developed active turbulence defined through a nonequilibrium phase transition
- **Authors:** Lasse Bonn, Tianxiang Ma, Olga Bantysh, Wei Feng, Martin Cramer Pedersen, Guangyin Jing, Jordi Ignés-Mullol, Francesc Sagués, Nuno A. M. Araujo, Amin Doostmohammadi
- **Submitted:** 2026-09-30 (accepted *Nature Physics*)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39348
- **Why newsworthy:** Critical activity where vorticity nodal lines percolate (SLE₆ / critical percolation) and vortex centres form a rigid system-spanning cluster — an experimentally accessible definition of fully developed active turbulence (MT–kinesin + bacteria + simulations).
- **Beginner hook:** When opposite-spin regions knit into one system-spanning web, active turbulence has crossed a critical line.
- **Caveat:** Active-nematic / bacterial experiments + simulations.

### 3. A Reynolds-like criterion for the onset of universal active turbulence
- **Authors:** Kunal Kumar, Sandip Sahoo, Kirti Kashyap, Amal Manoharan, Siddhartha Mukherjee, Anupam Gupta, Samriddhi Sankar Ray
- **Submitted:** 2026-09-30
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39999
- **Why newsworthy:** In TTSH dynamics, the universal spectrum E(k)∼k⁻³/² is reachable via multiple parameter routes that collapse onto a single Reynolds-like Re_act threshold.
- **Beginner hook:** One dial can mark membership in the universal active-turbulence club even when you turn different knobs.
- **Caveat:** Continuum theory / numerics.

### 4. Curvature-induced migration of small domains on lipid membranes
- **Authors:** Jaime Agudo-Canalejo
- **Submitted:** 2026-09-30
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39772
- **Why newsworthy:** Small-domain energy on fixed curved membranes; size-dependent line-tension coupling can switch preferred seats (e.g. poles → equator on oblates) as domains grow and coalesce.
- **Beginner hook:** Shape alone seats membrane islands — and growing bigger can move your preferred seat.
- **Caveat:** Theory (Biophysical Journal Helfrich special-issue invite).

### 5. Training and memory in a randomly driven fractal gel
- **Authors:** Chloe W. Lindeman, Joshua D. Clugston, Justin C. Goodrich, Mark Sutton, James L. Harden, Robert L. Leheny
- **Submitted:** 2026-09-30
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40010
- **Why newsworthy:** XPCS shows both cyclic and random shear train microstructural reversibility in a fractal nanoparticle gel; random-drive memory is readable with a standard amplitude protocol.
- **Beginner hook:** Messy random shaking can still teach a soft gel to remember how hard it was shoved.
- **Caveat:** Synchrotron XPCS / shear-cell experiment.
