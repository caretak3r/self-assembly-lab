## Highlights

### 1. Morphology and depletion force-based large-scale self-assembly of nanocubes on surface (MIDAS)
- **Authors:** Yeonhee Lee, Seungsang Cha, Yuna Kwak, Nicholas Juntunen, Grant M. Rotskoff, Jwa-Min Nam
- **Submitted:** 2026-09-09
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09761
- **Why newsworthy:** MIDAS uses morphology-sensitive depletion forces plus substrate roughness to build large-scale 2D monolayers or 3D multilayers of gold nanocubes without complex ligand recipes; lattice-gas and kinetic Monte Carlo modeling back the story.
- **Beginner hook:** Surface roughness and particle shape can organize nanoparticles as much as fancy coatings.
- **Caveat:** Research-lab nanoparticle work — not a classroom wet lab.

### 2. Designing corrugated surfaces to guide colloidal self-assembly
- **Authors:** Dinesh Kumar Sahu, Jude Ann Vishnu, Lisa Shafroth, Martin Lenz, Olivia du Roure, Julien Heuvingh
- **Submitted:** 2026-09-03
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04001
- **Why newsworthy:** Two-photon-printed side topography on polygonal colloids programs selective depletion assembly into dimers, chains, zigzags, or honeycombs; salt/depletant/topography scale flip selective ↔ non-selective regimes.
- **Beginner hook:** “Program the edges, get the structure” — LEGO-stud intuition for soft matter.
- **Open problems:** Tuning interaction specificity vs aggregate size in a reversible, scalable way.

### 3. Learning to build covering structures with continuous adjustments
- **Authors:** Gabriel Vallat, Maryam Kamgarpour, Stefana Parascho
- **Submitted:** 2026-09-08 · Accepted IROS 2026
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08669
- **Why newsworthy:** Hybrid RL (HSAC) adapts multi-robot block construction under fabrication noise instead of brittle fixed plans; physical two-robot spanning-arch demo with 3D-printed blocks.
- **Beginner hook:** Self-assembly-adjacent robotics — plans that tolerate mess, not only perfect CAD.

### 4. Machine learned designs of functional colloidal foldamers
- **Authors:** Ryan van Mastrigt, Zorana Zeravcic
- **Submitted:** 2026-08-28
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28554
- **Why newsworthy:** Reinforcement learning designs *pathways* (downhill vs detour) for DNA-coated droplet-chain folding; function can emerge from the process, including reconfiguration between structures.
- **Beginner hook:** Design the recipe over time — closer to protein folding than snapping bricks.

### 5. Mechanistic Framework for Multicomponent Nanoparticle Assembly (FormLNP)
- **Authors:** Turash Haque Pial, Tine Curk
- **Submitted:** 2026-09-03
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04029
- **Why newsworthy:** Process-aware kinetic Monte Carlo predicts single-particle heterogeneity for mRNA lipid nanoparticles and PEI–DNA complexes; open-source FormLNP tooling claimed in the abstract.
- **Beginner hook:** Vaccine and gene-delivery particles are self-assembled — process history shapes what each particle contains.

### 6. Towards AI-Driven Nanomedicine Discovery (NSA-Bench / NSA-Net)
- **Authors:** Quan Hao, Mengyue Fan, Zifan Dong, et al.
- **Submitted:** 2026-09-03
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04278
- **Why newsworthy:** Public NSA-Bench benchmark plus multimodal NSA-Net for predicting whether molecular pairs will self-assemble into nanomedicine candidates.
- **Beginner hook:** ML meets “will these molecules assemble?” — useful for data-curious students, with wet-lab caveats.

### Stretch (robotics, ~4 weeks)
- **Reconfiguration-Complete Motion Primitives for Deformable Planar Modular Robots** — Gu et al., submitted 2026-08-18 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17324 — constructive completeness results for pivot/shear primitives on deformable rhombus modules.

## Safe classroom / discussion follow-ons

1. **Corrugated-edge paper craft:** Identical cardboard hexagons; roughen only selected edges. Ask which patterns favor dimers vs chains vs loops (weak tape/magnets only — no chemicals). Inspired by arXiv:2609.04001.
2. **Pathway vs target journaling:** Reach the same square graph two ways — only add edges, or allow one undo — and discuss when undoing is necessary (echo of foldamer pathways, arXiv:2608.28554).
